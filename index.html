    Questions

    Will my customers know they're talking to an assistant?

    Yes. Prex introduces itself as your business's assistant, and a customer can ask for a person at any time.

    Does Prex replace my team?

    No. It answers first, collects the details and books the next step. Your team still talks to the customer and closes the deal.

    Which WhatsApp number does it use?

    Your business's own number, connected to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We set it up with you.

    Which languages does it speak?

    Prex replies in the language your customer writes in, including English, Arabic, Hindi and Nepali. We test every language with you during setup before it goes live.

    What if a customer asks something Prex doesn't know?

    It says so politely and passes the question to your team. It never makes up prices, availability or advice.

    What does it cost?

    The pilot is free. Founding clients get setup and their first month free. After that, there's a simple monthly plan, and we agree the price with you before anything is charged.
