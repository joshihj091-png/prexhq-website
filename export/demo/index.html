Made the old way

People in the Himalaya have chewed hard cheese like this for centuries. We make ours the same way, in small batches.

  1. 1

    Fresh milk

    Yak and cow milk is collected from herders in the hills.

  2. 2

    Pressed into bars

    The milk is curdled with lime juice and a little salt, then pressed into firm blocks.

  3. 3

    Dried for weeks

    Slow drying makes each bar rock-hard, so one chew keeps a dog busy for a long time.

Why dogs and owners like it

  • Long-lasting. Hard enough to keep a dog busy.
  • Short ingredient list. Yak milk, cow milk, salt, lime juice.
  • No grain or gluten. A simple treat for most dogs.
  • Nothing wasted. Microwave the last small piece until it puffs up, let it cool, and serve it as a crunchy treat.
